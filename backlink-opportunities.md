# MundoSaber Backlink Opportunities — Research Report
**Date:** 2026-10-13 | **Site:** https://mundosaber-gepc.onrender.com (ES+PT educational content)
**Method:** Fresh web research (Oct 2026 sources). DA = approximate Moz Domain Authority.
**Note on link types:** Social/profile platforms (Medium, LinkedIn, Pinterest, X, Instagram) apply `rel="nofollow"` to outbound links by default. Their value is trust, discovery, referral traffic, and entity signals — not direct link equity. Dofollow-capable platforms are marked. Claims of "dofollow" on social sites in SEO listicles are often inaccurate; this report uses the conservative (verified) value.

## TIER 1 — Do now (low risk, new-account safe)

### 1. Medium — medium.com (DA ~95)
- **How:** Publish long-form articles in Spanish (and Portuguese). Author bio + contextual links inside articles.
- **Link type:** Nofollow, but very high trust + strong Google indexing and Discover pickup.
- **Policy:** Original, high-quality content required. No spam, no excessive self-promotion, no duplicate/spun content. One quality article per topic is fine.
- **Risk:** LOW for new accounts if content is genuine and original.
- **Fit:** Perfect for Educación/Salud/Fe long-form repurposing. Spanish content performs well.

### 2. Blogger — blogger.com (DA ~76–92)
- **How:** Create a free Spanish blog (e.g., mundosaber-blog.blogspot.com), publish original posts with contextual dofollow links.
- **Link type:** Dofollow. Google-owned = fast indexing.
- **Policy:** Google's standard content policies; no spam, no deceptive practices. Keep posts original (do NOT copy-paste site articles verbatim — rewrite/summarize).
- **Risk:** LOW. Same Google account family as the site owner is normal usage.
- **Fit:** Ideal companion blog; Spanish-language Blogger community is large.

### 3. WordPress.com — wordpress.com (DA ~93)
- **How:** Free blog on a subdomain, publish original Spanish/Portuguese posts with contextual links.
- **Link type:** Dofollow-capable (links in post content pass equity).
- **Policy:** No spam blogs ("splogs"). Too many promotional links too fast = suspension. Publish real content, link sparingly and contextually.
- **Risk:** LOW-MEDIUM for new accounts (safe if content is real and link density is low).
- **Fit:** Great for all 5 niches; strong SEO footprint.

### 4. LinkedIn — linkedin.com (DA ~99)
- **How:** (a) LinkedIn Company Page for MundoSaber with website link; (b) long-form LinkedIn Articles in Spanish/Portuguese under a personal profile, with contextual links.
- **Link type:** Nofollow, but DA 99 trust + professional/education audience.
- **Policy:** Professional content expected. Articles must be substantive; blatant SEO spam articles get downranked. Company pages are explicitly for businesses.
- **Risk:** LOW. Company pages are a normal, expected use.
- **Fit:** Educación and Salud professional angle; Portuguese business audience is large on LinkedIn.

### 5. Tumblr — tumblr.com (DA ~88–90)
- **How:** Create a MundoSaber microblog; reblog/post short Spanish content with links back to full articles.
- **Link type:** Dofollow-capable on profile/post links (redirects apply in some placements).
- **Policy:** Lenient on links; spam blogs get terminated. Keep it active with real posts.
- **Risk:** LOW for new accounts.
- **Fit:** Curiosidades (fun facts) and Noticias Virales fit Tumblr's culture well.

### 6. Substack — substack.com (DA ~90+)
- **How:** Start a free MundoSaber newsletter (Spanish); each issue links back to site articles.
- **Link type:** Links in newsletters are followed; also builds an owned email list.
- **Policy:** Newsletters are the core product — links expected and welcome. No spam restrictions beyond normal anti-abuse.
- **Risk:** LOW.
- **Fit:** Fe (devotionals) and Educación (study tips) are natural newsletter content.

## TIER 2 — Do next (medium risk or more effort)

### 7. Quora — quora.com / es.quora.com / pt.quora.com (DA ~93)
- **How:** Complete profile (in progress) → answer questions in Spanish/Portuguese in our niches with genuinely helpful 250+ word answers; drop site links only as additional resources, never with a call-to-action.
- **Link type:** Profile link + contextual answer links (high trust; answers rank in Google).
- **Policy (strict):** No affiliate links. No CTAs ("click here"). No sales pitches. No duplicate content. Blatant self-promotion = downvotes/ban. Links must genuinely add value to the answer.
- **Risk:** MEDIUM for new accounts (safe if the first 10–15 answers have zero or minimal links and are genuinely helpful).
- **Fit:** Excellent for Salud (health questions), Educación (study questions), Fe. Huge Spanish/Portuguese Q&A volume.

### 8. SlideShare — slideshare.net (DA ~95)
- **How:** Convert article summaries into Spanish slide decks (Canva/PowerPoint), upload with article link in description.
- **Link type:** Links in descriptions; decks rank in Google.
- **Policy:** Real presentations required; spam uploads removed. Educational content is the platform's bread and butter.
- **Risk:** LOW-MEDIUM.
- **Fit:** Educación (study techniques) and Salud decks; LinkedIn-owned = professional audience.

### 9. Flipboard — flipboard.com (DA ~92)
- **How:** Create MundoSaber magazines per category; flip (curate) site articles plus third-party content.
- **Link type:** Nofollow, but strong discovery + referral traffic.
- **Policy:** Curation platform — mixing own + others' content is the expected behavior. Pure self-promotion magazines get low distribution.
- **Risk:** LOW.
- **Fit:** Noticias Virales and Curiosidades magazines.

### 10. Behance — behance.net (DA ~92)
- **How:** Publish infographic-style projects (from article images) with project links to articles.
- **Link type:** Profile + project links (high trust).
- **Policy:** Portfolio platform; projects must be genuine creative work. Infographics/data-visualization are welcome.
- **Risk:** LOW.
- **Fit:** Only worth it if producing infographics from article data (Salud stats, Curiosidades facts).

### 11. Reddit — reddit.com (DA ~91)
- **How:** ONLY after 60+ days of genuine participation. Relevant subs: r/es, r/espanol, r/portugues, r/brasil, plus niche subs. 90/10 rule: 9 genuine contributions per 1 self-promotional mention.
- **Link type:** Mostly nofollow/UGC, but fastest indexing + huge referral traffic when a post lands.
- **Policy (very strict):** Each subreddit has its own rules; new accounts posting links = ~90% ban rate within 30 days per 2026 practitioner data. Never cross-post the same link to multiple subs.
- **Risk:** HIGH for new accounts. Treat as a 2–3 month project, not a quick win.
- **Fit:** Long-term only. Do NOT start link-dropping now.
